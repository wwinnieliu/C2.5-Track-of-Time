Track of Time Story Key Interaction

Story

A little tapping on the foot, constantly look around for the train to come. A sense of voice is telling you that this is a waste of time. But what if it’s not?

Waiting is something people see as dead time when it doesn’t have to be, as well as the idea that strangers you see at the station are all waiting together but never actually together. A platform is full of people sharing the same delay, the same annoyance, and the same boredom, and almost no one acknowledges it.
